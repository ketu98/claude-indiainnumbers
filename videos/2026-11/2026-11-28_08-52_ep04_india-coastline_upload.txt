TITLE
India's coastline is NOT 7,516 km. It's 11,098 km 🌊

DESCRIPTION
India's coastline was re-measured: 11,098.81 km, up from the 7,516.6 km figure used since the 1970s, about 48% longer with no new land. Gujarat's coast nearly doubled to 2,340.62 km and West Bengal's grew 4.6x. Which coastal state would you visit first?
