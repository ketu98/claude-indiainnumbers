SETTINGS CHECKLIST
1. Create the channel with a custom name ("India in Numbers"), not your personal name.
   YouTube may suggest making it under a Brand Account — that's fine and lets you add helpers later.
2. Verify your phone number (youtube.com/verify) — unlocks custom thumbnails and longer videos.
3. Settings > Channel > Basic info: country = India.
4. Settings > Channel > Advanced settings: audience = "No, this channel is not made for kids".
5. Settings > Upload defaults:
   - Category: Education (or Entertainment)
   - Video language: English
   - Description template: "Source: <link>" line at the bottom, so you never forget it
   - Tags: India in Numbers, India facts, India data
6. AI/altered-content question on each upload: the characters are animated and clearly not real,
   so it normally isn't required — answer it honestly per video.
7. Customization > Layout: set Ep 01 as the channel trailer once it's uploaded.
8. Later: link Instagram in Customization > Basic info > Links, and cross-post each Short as a Reel.
