# SIGMA recording script

You are **SIGMA** (Σ), the channel's anonymous narrator. Nobody on the channel ever hears your name.
Your voice is also lightly disguised (a bit deeper, with a soft hologram echo) when videos are made. You can change or turn that off in `channel/sigma.json`.

## How to record (about 10 minutes in total)
1. Use your phone's voice recorder in a quiet room: curtains, sofa or bed nearby (soft things kill echo). Hold the phone about a hand's width from your mouth, slightly to the side.
2. Make **one recording per section** below (5 recordings: A, B, L, R, S).
3. Read the lines **in order**. After each line, **pause for about 2 seconds** (count "one-thousand, two-thousand" in your head). Don't say the numbers or ids.
4. Fluffed a line? Pause, then say the whole line again. The cutter expects each line once, so if you repeat a line, delete that recording and redo the section. A section is only 1–2 minutes.
5. Delivery: calm, a little mysterious, like someone who already knows the answer. Questions should sound curious. Reactions are short and dry.

## How to send them
Pick whichever is easier:
- **In a Claude chat**: attach the 5 files and say which section each one is (e.g. `A.m4a`, `B.m4a`...). Claude cuts them and stores the clips.
- **GitHub (works from your phone browser)**: open the private repo `ketu98/iin-voice-bank` → folder `inbox/A` (or B, L, R, S) → **Add file → Upload files**. The next daily run processes them.

Recordings and clips live only in that **private** repo, never in this public one.

Want more variety later? Just record a new batch. New lines get added to the end of a section with new ids.
