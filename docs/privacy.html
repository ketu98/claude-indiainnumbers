Privacy policy: India in Numbers uploader

Last updated: 27 November 2026

"India in Numbers uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel India in Numbers to upload that channel's own videos. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

  • With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), it uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channel's videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and publishing times, add them to the channel's playlist, and read their status and view counts.
  • It does not access, collect or store any data about viewers or any other Google user.

Storage and sharing

  • The authorization token is stored as an encrypted secret in the channel's private automation settings and is used only for the actions above.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
