# Topic backlog

Pick any unused topic (check episodes/LOG.md). Categories are in brackets so runs can avoid repeating the same category back to back.
Source hints are starting points; always verify the latest figures.

## Cities [cities]
- Which Indian metro gets the most rain in a year? (IMD normals for Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Delhi)
- Which city has the cleanest air: Bengaluru, Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai or Kolkata? (CPCB annual AQI / city reports)
- How long does a Mumbai local train carry passengers each day? (Central / Western Railway figures)
- Which Indian airport is the busiest? (AAI annual traffic statistics)
- How many people use the Delhi Metro every day? (DMRC ridership)

## Money & everyday prices [prices]  (facts only, never advice)
- What did a litre of petrol cost in 2000 vs today? (PPAC historical prices)
- How much did a movie ticket cost 20 years ago? (industry reports, FICCI-EY media report)
- What is India's most expensive vegetable this month? (Dept of Consumer Affairs price monitoring)
- How much gold do Indians buy in a year? (World Gold Council India reports)

## Digital India [digital]
- How many UPI payments happen every second? (done: ep02. Repeat only with a new angle, e.g. biggest UPI day ever)
- How many Indians are online? (TRAI subscription data)
- How cheap is mobile data in India vs the world? (cable.co.uk worldwide mobile data pricing)
- How many FASTag toll payments happen per day? (NPCI NETC statistics)
- What do Indians search for most on Google? (Google Year in Search India)

## Food [food]
