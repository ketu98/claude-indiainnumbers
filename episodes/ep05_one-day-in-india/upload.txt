TITLE
One Day in India in Numbers: Births, Milk, Trains, UPI & Power 🇮🇳

DESCRIPTION
What does one single day in India look like? The panel follows a full 24 hours through official data: births, breakfast, commutes, money, electricity and mobile data.

The standouts: about 69,000 births registered every day (2023), about 2 crore train journeys a day, and 82 crore UPI payments a day in October 2026, worth over ₹1 lakh crore. Peak power demand has almost doubled since 2013-14.

Which of these numbers surprised you most? Tell us in the comments.
